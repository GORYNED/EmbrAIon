# Learning

Learning is a reviewed promotion workflow:

observation → candidate → evidence accumulation → proposal → review → approved promotion

## Sequence

1. Runtime or eval evidence identifies a repeated pattern.
2. The learning tool creates or updates a privacy-safe candidate.
3. Evidence accumulates across independent runs or contexts.
4. A proposal identifies the narrowest reusable target. A knowledge audit may suggest stale or missing bindings, but remains advisory: it does not rewrite project knowledge or Core automatically. For a proposed reusable skill or trigger change, apply skill-authoring and compare the live baseline with the candidate, including positive and near-negative cases in English and Russian.
5. Reviewer and, when relevant, Architect or Steward assess generality and risk.
6. A human or configured governance authority approves promotion.
7. The promoted capability is implemented through the ordinary engineering workflow.
8. Evals and validation verify the new capability.

Learning never directly edits canonical Core.
