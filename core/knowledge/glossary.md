# Glossary

- **Lead** — orchestration and protected-decision owner.
- **Worker** — bounded writable implementer.
- **Reviewer** — independent read-only reviewer.
- **Architect** — architecture and ownership specialist.
- **Analyst** — intent, requirement, and analysis specialist.
- **Validator** — validation planning and evidence specialist.
- **Researcher** — bounded read-only research specialist.
- **Steward** — persistence and compatibility custodian.
- **Adapter** — host/provider-specific integration.
- **Projection** — host-specific representation derived from canonical policy.
