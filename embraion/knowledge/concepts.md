# Concepts

## Project overlay

Project-owned policy and knowledge layered on top of EmbrAIon without weakening Core hard rules.

## Projection

A host-specific generated or adapted representation of canonical Core content.

## Route class

A provider-neutral capability tier selected by Core routing and resolved to concrete models by an adapter.

## Protected decision

A decision whose security, migration, recovery, or systemic risk requires elevated authority and explicit evidence.
