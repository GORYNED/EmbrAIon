# Brand Voice

EmbrAIon writing should be:

- technical;
- concise;
- evidence-oriented;
- modern and minimal;
- explicit about ownership, validation, and limitations.

Avoid generic AI hype, vague claims of intelligence, and decorative terminology inside technical documentation.

Use the fire/spark metaphor for brand-level communication. Use standard engineering language for architecture, routing, validation, security, tooling, and implementation.

## Preferred pattern

Lead with the concrete system or behavior, then explain why it exists.

## Naming

Use **EmbrAIon** for the engineering system/framework.

Use **GORYNED** as the creator and parent brand.
